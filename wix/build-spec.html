Step 00

Set the palette and fonts first

Do this before touching any section. In Wix these are global — set them once and every element you add afterwards inherits them, instead of you recoloring each text box by hand.

Site colors → Change site palette

  • #0a0e17Page background
  • #10151fAlternating sections
  • #161c29Cards, panels
  • #1a66e8Buttons
  • #7fb0ffLabels, prices
  • #f6f8fbHeadings
  • #c7cedbBody text
  • #8b96a9Secondary text

Site fonts → Change text theme

RoleFontWeight & treatment
All headingsJura700, tight line height
Body textJura400, 16px, line height 1.6
Small labelsIBM Plex Mono500, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.14em

Both fonts are in Wix's library. Search them by name in the font picker — no upload needed. The little uppercase labels above each heading ("Services & Pricing", "Recent Work") are the mono font; everything else is Jura.

Buttons

Fill #1a66e8, white text, fully rounded corners (pill shape), no border. Secondary buttons: transparent fill, white text, 1px border at rgba(255,255,255,.16).

FAQ

Wix has an accordion element — use it, and leave every item collapsed by default.

Section heading

GOOD TO KNOW
Frequently asked questions.

Question 1

What do you need from me on the day?

Just access to an outdoor water tap and a standard power outlet at the address. If either isn't available, let us know when you book and we'll figure out a plan.

Question 2

Do I need to be home during the appointment?

Not necessarily — as long as we can access the vehicle, water, and power, plenty of customers leave us to it. We'll confirm the details with you when you book.

Question 3

What happens if it's raining?

Exterior work is weather-dependent — if rain is forecast for your appointment, we'll reach out to reschedule. Interior-only details can often still go ahead under a carport or garage.

Question 4

How much does it actually cost?

Every package above lists pricing by vehicle size, pulled straight from our booking system. Distance from Agassiz can adjust the total slightly — you'll see the exact price before you confirm at checkout.

Question 5

Do you travel outside the Fraser Valley?

Our regular coverage is Agassiz, Harrison Hot Springs, Rosedale, Popkum, Chilliwack, and Hope — see the Service Area section above. We also take bookings across Greater Vancouver by appointment. Outside that, get in touch and we'll let you know if it's workable.

Question 6

How do I book?

Easiest way is our online shop — pick a package, choose your vehicle size, and book a time. Prefer to talk it through first? Call or text +1 877 552 8664, or email info@jakescardetailing.ca.

Last

Moving the domain

Only once you're happy with the built site — there's no rush, and nothing breaks while you work.

jakescardetailing.ca is currently connected to your other Wix site, the Premium one. Because both sites are inside the same Wix account, this is a reassignment, not a DNS change — your nameservers stay exactly as they are.

  • Publish the new site and click through it on a phone as well as a laptop.
  • Send yourself a test through the quote form and confirm it arrives.
  • Move the Premium plan from the old site to this one, then connect the domain.
  • Keep the old site — unpublished, not deleted — for a few weeks as a fallback.
Don't delete anything until the new site has been live on the domain for a week or two. Your GitHub original stays online at its github.io address regardless, so you always have the reference to compare against.

Every price, caption, and paragraph here was read directly out of your existing site's source, so it matches what's live today rather than being retyped from memory. The two deliberate departures from the original — showing the price tables instead of hiding them behind a toggle, and building the quote form so it actually sends — are both flagged in place above.
